# 插画来源

`bakery.webp` 为本次使用内置 imagegen 工具生成的虚构场景，经本地 WebP 编码与网页尺寸优化；未使用真实商家、外链图片或人物照片。最终项目素材路径：`prototype/assets/bakery.webp`。

生成提示词：

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: opening scene art for a Chinese browser narrative investigation game. Create one wide landscape 3:2 editorial graphic-novel illustration of a small contemporary Chinese neighborhood bakery on a grey morning. Several ordinary customers queue outside a glass shopfront, bread trays visible inside, a young employee in a dark apron standing near the door looking worried while the business appears popular. Beautiful restrained ink linework, subtle screenprint grain, architectural composition, cool light grey and charcoal with a single muted vermilion red awning accent, no other saturated colors. Human-scale urban street, no dramatic crime props. Scene fills frame. No words, no lettering, no labels, no text, no watermark. Polished atmospheric visual storytelling, not a website mockup.

开场采用 `design-taste-frontend` 技能中的布局、对比度、移动端及无障碍原则。游戏交互使用原生浏览器控件；视觉是调查档案美学，不声称使用某个官方设计系统。设计参数为布局变化6、动态3、密度4。
